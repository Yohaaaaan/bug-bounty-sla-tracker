Privacy Policy & Terms of Use

1. Privacy Policy & Data Collection

We respect your privacy. This website operates on a "Privacy-First" basis. We do not use intrusive tracking cookies, third-party analytics (like Google Analytics), or fingerprinting.

Data Collection: We collect strictly necessary data to prevent abuse (such as hashed IP addresses with a daily rotating salt). This data cannot be reverse-engineered to identify you and is only used to calculate basic traffic statistics.

2. Content & Safe Harbor

This platform is a community-driven aggregator of purely factual SLA (Service Level Agreement) metadata. We act strictly as a hosting provider (Safe Harbor) and do not editorialize or create the content submitted by users.

Takedown Policy & Contact: If you represent an organization and believe a report is demonstrably false, defamatory, or contains sensitive PII/vulnerability details, you can request a manual review and takedown by contacting us at: yohanmichau1@gmail.com.

3. Anti-Spam (Proof-of-Work)

To prevent automated abuse without using invasive captchas (like reCAPTCHA), we utilize a client-side cryptographic Proof-of-Work (PoW). Your device computes a hash to validate the submission.

4. Community Moderation

Reports are self-moderated by the community. Any report receiving a threshold of downvotes is automatically quarantined and hidden from the public feed.

5. Open Source & Liability

This tool is provided "as is", without warranty of any kind. We are not affiliated with any bug bounty platforms or companies listed in the database.
